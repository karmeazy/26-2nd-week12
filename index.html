The School Remains

Through maps and data visualization,
this project explores school closures
and the changing status of former school sites.
It draws attention to the gap between a school closing
its doors and its space finding a new role
—and to the places still waiting.

As the school-age population declines,
schools in Seoul are closing or merging,
leaving behind spaces that once served their communities.
While some find new uses,
others remain without a clear purpose.
